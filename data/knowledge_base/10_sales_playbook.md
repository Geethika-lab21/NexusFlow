# NexusFlow Sales Playbook & Qualification Framework

## BANT Qualification Criteria

### 1. Budget
- **Under $5k**: Self-serve starter candidate; direct to self-paced onboarding and documentation.
- **$5k–$20k**: Standard Professional tier; focus on rapid time to value and 20% annual discount incentive.
- **$20k–$50k+**: Mid-market opportunity; position dedicated CSM and bi-directional CRM integration.
- **$100k+**: Enterprise strategic account; engage Enterprise Account Executive and Solutions Architect.

### 2. Authority
- Confirm whether contact holds budget sign-off authority (VP of Sales, Head of RevOps, CTO, COO).
- If prospect is an individual SDR or analyst, identify their manager and provide internal business case collateral.

### 3. Need
- Identify top operational bottleneck: manual CRM triage, slow response to web leads, or inconsistent follow-up messaging.
- Map the identified pain point to NexusFlow automated capabilities.

### 4. Timeline
- **Immediate (< 1 month)**: Expedite live architecture review and customized trial setup.
- **1–3 months**: Schedule standard demo followed by ROI calculation review.
- **6+ months**: Enroll in nurture cadence with quarterly product updates and benchmark reports.

## Discovery Questions
- "How many hours does your team currently spend manually prioritizing and drafting responses to incoming leads?"
- "What happens when an inbound lead submits a question regarding complex security compliance or competitor differences?"
- "How do your sales managers enforce quality and adherence to messaging playbooks across the sales team?"
