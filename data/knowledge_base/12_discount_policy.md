# NexusFlow Pricing & Discount Authority Policy

## Discount Authorization Matrix

| Discount Level | Required Approval Authority | Valid Conditions & Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0% – 10%** | Individual SDR / Account Executive | Annual upfront contract commitment or early quarterly signing. |
| **10% – 25%** | Sales Manager / Director of Sales | Minimum 10+ seats, multi-year term (2+ years), or strategic brand logo. |
| **> 25%** | VP of Global Sales / Chief Revenue Officer | Enterprise contracts exceeding $50k ARR, case study participation commitment. |

## Standard Terms & Payment Requirements
- Standard payment terms are Net 30 for Enterprise invoices and immediate credit card / ACH for Starter and Professional tiers.
- Non-standard SLA penalty credits or bespoke data residency guarantees require legal counsel sign-off in addition to sales management approval.
- Month-to-month contracts are not eligible for discretionary discounts beyond the standard published tier pricing.
