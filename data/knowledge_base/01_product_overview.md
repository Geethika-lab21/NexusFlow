# NexusFlow Product Overview

## Mission & Vision
NexusFlow is an intelligent revenue orchestration and sales workflow automation platform built for modern B2B revenue teams. NexusFlow combines event-driven workflow automation, predictive machine learning lead scoring, and agentic AI to bridge the gap between sales development representatives (SDRs), account executives (AEs), and customer success teams.

## Core Value Propositions
1. **Intelligent Lead Triage**: Eliminate manual CRM data entry and subjective prioritization with calibrated ML conversion predictions and multi-criteria scoring.
2. **Context-Aware Follow-Up Generation**: Automatically synthesize historical interactions, company pain points, and product documentation into personalized, policy-compliant outreach.
3. **Automated Objection Handling**: Surface verified sales collateral and case studies instantaneously via hybrid RAG retrieval when prospects express hesitation.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Governance**: Provide granular control to sales leadership with mandatory approval workflows before any customer-facing communication is dispatched.
5. **Universal Ecosystem Connectivity**: Seamlessly bi-directionally sync with over 200 enterprise systems, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Jira.
