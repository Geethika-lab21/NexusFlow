# NexusFlow Feature Documentation

## 1. Event-Driven Workflow Automation
- **Visual Canvas Builder**: Drag-and-drop trigger and action canvas supporting complex branching logic, multi-stage approvals, and failure fallbacks.
- **Webhook & Polling Triggers**: Ingest real-time webhook payloads from custom web applications, marketing platforms, and billing engines.
- **Batch Data Pipelines**: Scheduled batch executions for mass enrichment, lead recalculation, and CRM sync operations.

## 2. Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics
- **Supervised Conversion Scoring**: Real-time classification trained on historical pipeline outcomes predicting lead win probability with calibrated confidence intervals.
- **Dynamic Heuristic Prioritization**: Configurable multi-criteria scoring combining engagement velocity, budget alignment, decision-maker authority, and timeline urgency.
- **Explainable Feature Contributions**: Granular breakdown of positive, neutral, and negative scoring factors for complete rep transparency.

## 3. Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)
- **Hybrid Search Engine**: Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) coupling dense semantic embeddings with sparse BM25 lexical keyword matching.
- **Auto-Chunking & Ingestion**: Ingest PDF, Markdown, DOCX, and TXT collateral with automated SHA-256 deduplication and metadata extraction.
- **Source Citation Safeguards**: Outreach drafts embed verified source chunk tags to prevent AI hallucinations.

## 4. Agentic AI & Human-in-the-Loop Orchestration
- **Autonomous SDR Workflows**: Evaluates lead readiness, retrieves relevant collateral, determines strategic next actions, and formulates tailored outreach copy.
- **Review & Approval Gateways**: Integrated managerial approval inbox preventing unauthorized email or message dispatch.
- **Audit Trails & Step Tracing**: Comprehensive logging capturing node transition timestamps, tool inputs, and intermediate outputs.
