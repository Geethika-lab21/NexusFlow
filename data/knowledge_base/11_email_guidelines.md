# NexusFlow Email Communication Guidelines

## Voice & Tone Best Practices
- **Empathetic & Professional**: Address the prospect's operational pain points without condescension or hyperbole.
- **Concise & Direct**: Keep introductory emails between 75 and 150 words. Prospects review outreach on mobile devices.
- **Evidence-Backed**: Reference specific company context, pain points, or previous interactions.
- **Single Clear Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Propose a specific, friction-free next step (e.g., "Are you open to a brief 15-minute walkthrough this Thursday at 2 PM?").

## Subject Line Guidelines
- Keep subject lines under 45 characters to prevent truncation on mobile screens.
- Use personalized variables: `{first_name}`, `{company}`, or `{pain_point_keyword}`.
- Avoid spam trigger words ("Act Now!", "Guaranteed", "Free $$$", all caps).

## Recommended Follow-Up Cadence
- **Day 1**: Initial personalized outreach acknowledging form submission or demo request.
- **Day 3**: Follow-up providing relevant case study or technical documentation chunk.
- **Day 7**: Value-oriented objection check-in regarding timeline or team priorities.
- **Day 14**: Graceful "breakup" email offering an open-door resource link.
- **Policy Limit**: Maximum 2 outbound follow-ups per 7-day period to prevent domain reputation fatigue.
