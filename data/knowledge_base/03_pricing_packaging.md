# NexusFlow Pricing & Packaging

## Plan Breakdown

### Starter Plan
- **Price**: $49 per user / month (billed monthly) or $39 per user / month (billed annually, 20% discount).
- **Target Audience**: Seed-stage startups and small sales teams up to 5 seats.
- **Inclusions**:
  - Up to 2,500 active leads.
  - Basic rule-based workflow automation.
  - Standard CRM integrations (HubSpot, Google Sheets).
  - Community support and weekly office hours.
  - Standard email outreach templates.

### Professional Plan
- **Price**: $149 per user / month (billed monthly) or $119 per user / month (billed annually, 20% discount).
- **Target Audience**: Growth-stage B2B scaleups and mid-market revenue organizations.
- **Inclusions**:
  - Up to 25,000 active leads.
  - Predictive Machine Learning conversion scoring.
  - Full hybrid RAG knowledge base search across up to 100 documents.
  - Autonomous AI SDR drafting with managerial approval gates.
  - Bi-directional sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Jira, and webhooks.
  - Priority email and Slack support with 4-hour response SLA.

### Enterprise Plan
- **Price**: Custom contract based on volume tiers and dedicated infrastructure requirements (typical starting entry at $1,200/month for 10+ seats).
- **Target Audience**: Large enterprises requiring strict compliance, high throughput, and bespoke integrations.
- **Inclusions**:
  - Unlimited active leads and data ingestion.
  - Fine-tuned custom ML models trained on private historical pipeline data.
  - SAML 2.0 / Okta / Azure AD Single Sign-On (SSO).
  - Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) and custom onboarding sprints.
  - 99.9% uptime SLA backed by contractual credits.
  - Custom data residency (US, EU, APAC regions).
