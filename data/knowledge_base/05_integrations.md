# NexusFlow Integrations & Connectivity

## Ecosystem Connectivity (200+ Connectors)
NexusFlow provides native pre-built connectors and robust webhook/API capabilities to interface with your existing revenue technology stack.

### CRM Systems
- **Salesforce**: Two-way real-time synchronization of Leads, Contacts, Accounts, and Opportunities. Custom field mapping, APEX trigger triggers, and Flow support.
- **HubSpot**: Seamless sync for Deals, Contacts, and Marketing Automation workflows.
- **Pipedrive & Microsoft Dynamics 365**: High-speed REST synchronization for pipeline stages and interaction logs.

### Communication & Productivity
- **Slack**: Channel notifications for hot leads, draft approvals, deal milestone alerts, and interactive slash commands (`/nexusflow score <email>`).
- **Microsoft Teams**: Adaptive cards for real-time approval buttons and pipeline anomaly alerts.
- **Gmail & Microsoft Outlook / Exchange**: Automated email thread tracking and message draft injection.

### Project & Engineering
- **Jira & Linear**: Automatically create engineering tickets when customer feature blockers or product gaps are flagged in sales calls.
- **Zapier & Make.com**: Fallback connector webhooks enabling integration with 5,000+ niche productivity tools.

### Developer API & Webhooks
- **RESTful API**: Full CRUD programmatic access to Leads, Scoring Endpoints, Agent Workflows, and Follow-Up Drafts.
- **Event Webhooks**: Configurable HMAC-SHA256 signed payloads dispatched on events like `lead.scored`, `draft.approved`, `run.completed`.
