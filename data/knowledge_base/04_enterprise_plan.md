# NexusFlow Enterprise Plan Overview

## Enterprise-Grade Capabilities

### Security & Identity
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: SAML 2.0, OpenID Connect, Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), Google Workspace.
- **SCIM Provisioning**: Automated user provisioning, deprovisioning, and team directory sync.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Custom roles with fine-grained permissions for SDRs, Account Executives, Sales Managers, and System Admins.

### Infrastructure & Reliability
- **Guaranteed Availability**: Contractual 99.9% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA).
- **Dedicated VPC Deployments**: Isolated database and compute environments hosted in AWS or GCP.
- **Data Residency Options**: Tenant isolation with storage locked to North America (US-East), Europe (Frankfurt), or Asia-Pacific (Tokyo).

### Dedicated Success & Support
- **Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)**: Quarterly business reviews (QBRs), customized adoption roadmaps, and executive check-ins.
- **Dedicated Solutions Architect**: Architecture advisory, bespoke webhook configurations, and legacy CRM migration assistance.
- **Priority Escalation Hotline**: 24/7/365 emergency hotline with 15-minute response SLA for critical system outages.
