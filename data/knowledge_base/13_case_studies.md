# NexusFlow Enterprise Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Omnichannel Retailer (Krypton Commerce)
- **Profile**: 1,200 employees, retail & e-commerce.
- **Challenge**: Over 400 weekly inbound wholesale lead inquiries were manually entered into Salesforce, resulting in a 48-hour response latency and 22% dropped leads.
- **Solution**: Deployed NexusFlow ML scoring and automatic draft generation for Tier 1 wholesale leads.
- **Results**:
  - Response time decreased from 48 hours to 18 minutes.
  - Inbound lead qualification conversion improved by 41%.
  - Saved 32 SDR hours weekly across an 8-person team.

## Case Study 2: Industrial Manufacturing (Universal Dynamics)
- **Profile**: 3,500 employees, precision manufacturing & supply chain.
- **Challenge**: Complex RFPs with technical specifications stalled because sales reps lacked fast access to product capability documentation.
- **Solution**: Ingested 250 engineering specifications and compliance documents into NexusFlow hybrid RAG engine.
- **Results**:
  - Technical inquiry response turnaround improved by 65%.
  - Win rate on enterprise bids increased from 24% to 37%.
  - Zero compliance or specification hallucinations reported over 6 months.

## Case Study 3: HealthTech Platform (Summit Care Solutions)
- **Profile**: 280 employees, healthcare digital health provider.
- **Challenge**: Strict HIPAA regulations and SOC 2 scrutiny slowed enterprise hospital sales cycles.
- **Solution**: Implemented NexusFlow Enterprise with SOC 2 Type II controls and Human-in-the-Loop review gates.
- **Results**:
  - 100% compliance adherence across all outbound enterprise communication.
  - Closed 14 hospital system enterprise contracts in two quarters.
  - Average sales cycle shortened from 110 days to 68 days.
